BM25 (Best Matching 25) is a ranking function used by search engines to estimate the relevance of documents to a
given search query. It is based on a bag-of-words retrieval model and takes into account term frequency, inverse
document frequency, and document length normalization. Because BM25 is purely lexical, it does not understand
synonyms or paraphrases, which is why combining it with dense embeddings often improves overall retrieval quality.
